Everyone finds viral products after they go viral. Here's how to find them before.

Three signals, ten minutes a day, and one prompt to check if it'll last.

[sigh] By the time it's everywhere, you're late.

Three signals. The last one confirms the trend.

Signal one, the search. On TikTok, search made me buy it. Filter to this week. Look for products with tons of views, but only a few sellers yet.

Signal two, the comments. Where do I buy this? Link? Does it ship to me? Every one of those is a buyer with no store to buy from.

And signal three? [question-oh]

Signal three, the trend. Type the product into Google Trends. A spike that's already falling? Skip it. A steady climb? You're early.

Then stress-test it. Ask AI: is this a fad or a trend? Who buys it, what problem does it solve, and how easy is it to copy?

Bonus. Test small. Order a few units, or run one cheap ad first. Viral today can be dead next month.

Don't chase viral. Spot it early.
