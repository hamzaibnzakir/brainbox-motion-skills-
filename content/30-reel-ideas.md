# AI for E-commerce — 30 Short-Form Video Scripts

**Niche:** AI for e-commerce store owners
**Platforms:** TikTok + Instagram Reels
**Cadence:** 2 posts/day (AM = quick win, PM = deeper piece) → 15 days of content
**Format:** 9:16, 20–45s, captions always on, hook on screen in the first frame

**Pillars**
- 🔍 Teardown: store audits
- 🕵️ Spy: competitor intelligence
- 🤖 Tool: one AI tool, one result
- ⚙️ Automation: agents and workflows
- ⚡ Quick win: fast, practical fixes

**Visual system (keep consistent):** dark background, one accent color, bold sans-serif kinetic text, screen recordings inside a floating phone or laptop frame, red circle and arrow callouts, a "ding" sound on every reveal.

## 01 — "I audited this store in 60 seconds"
**Pillar:** 🔍 Teardown | **Slot:** PM | **Length:** ~40s

**Hook (0–3s):** "This store gets traffic but no sales. Here's why in 60 seconds."

**Script:**
1. [Screen recording of the homepage, scrolling] "First problem: I can't tell what they sell in 3 seconds."
2. [Zoom to the hero section, red X] "The headline says 'Elevate your lifestyle.' That means nothing."
3. [Zoom to a product page] "No reviews above the fold. Shipping cost hidden until checkout."
4. [PageSpeed score animating to 31] "And it loads in 6 seconds on mobile. Half the visitors leave."
5. [Checklist animates in] "Fix these 3 things and conversions go up before you spend another dollar on ads."

**CTA:** "Want me to audit yours? Comment AUDIT."
**Caption:** Most stores don't need more traffic. They need fewer leaks. #shopify #ecommerce #ecommercetips
